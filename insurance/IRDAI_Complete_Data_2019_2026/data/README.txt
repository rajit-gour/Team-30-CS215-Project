TEAM 30 | IRDAI LIFE INSURANCE | SOURCE-BASED MONTHLY EXTRACTION

Main output: insurance_monthly_2019_2026.csv
93 chronological months January 2019 through September 2026, with monthly new individual policy counts split into single and non-single premium. All observations originated from supplied IRDAI workbooks (including .xls).

Corrections: recovery_and_correction_log.csv documents seven special cases. July 2022 is especially important: a workbook listed as July contains mislabeled June headers, but its numbers align with published July 2022 prior-year comparisons from a July 2023 workbook. The genuine June 2022 is taken from its own workbook.

CAVEATS: This is a candidate research dataset, not a full independent audit. Recovered fields have been arithmetically checked; time-series and insurer subtotal validation are still necessary. The detailed insurer file and other reported metrics are preliminary and may contain reference information from incorrectly labeled reports, so use them only after checking source cell provenance.

Files: source_file_map.csv maps temporary identifiers back to exact original archive filenames.
